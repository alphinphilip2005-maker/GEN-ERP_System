When is the Number Assigned?

Comparison of numbering strategies during the "Pending" phase.

A

Inherit Current Number

The pending record is named r26 (Pending).

  • Easy to see it's an update TO r26.
  • Sorting works naturally.
  • Needs logic to "rename" the row during approval.
B

The "Candidate" Approach

The record has NO number. It's just listed as "Proposed Change".

  • Pure logic: No r27 exists anywhere until it's Approved.
  • Hard to refer to a specific draft if multiple exist.
