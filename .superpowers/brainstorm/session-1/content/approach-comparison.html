BOM Revision Logic: When to Increment?

Select the approach that best fits your workflow

A

Immediate Versioning (Current)

Clicking "Release" immediately creates Revision 27 as a Pending record.

  • Clear visibility of the "Next" version.
  • User feels the number has updated too early.
B

Late Versioning (User Requested)

Clicking "Release" creates a Pending Update. The number r27 is only assigned when "Accept" is clicked.

  • Header strictly stays at r26 until it's actually Approved.
  • Drafts don't have a number, making them harder to track in history.
C

Snapshot Versioning

Release creates a "Candidate" (e.g. 26.1). Approval converts it to Revision 27.

  • Maintains strict r26 header while giving the draft a unique ID.
  • Adds more complexity to the numbering system.
